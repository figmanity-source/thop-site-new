STEP 02 · BIOBLUEPRINT
Before we change anything, we measure. Three simple tests show us how your body is really doing today.
TEST 1 · CELL SCORE
15 seconds
A fingertip scan. Rest your fingertip on a scanner for about 15 seconds. It reads your carotenoids: the antioxidants that help protect your cells. No blood. No needles.
TEST 2 · BLOOD PICTURE
one blood draw
One blood draw. We check a wide panel of longevity and stem cell markers. It shows how your metabolism, organs and cells are working right now.
TEST 3 · GENETIC BLUEPRINT
one cheek swab
A quick cheek swab. NutriDNA by Strand Life Sciences reads how your genes affect food, weight, exercise, inflammation and sleep. The full report runs to around 200 pages. We turn it into a few clear actions.
The fingertip scan uses patent-pending Spectral Rai Technology, taking more than 700,000 light measurements in one reading.

STEP 03 · DOX · NUTRITION DETOX & ANF LIVER CLEANSE
STEP 03 · DOX · NUTRITION DETOX & ANF LIVER CLEANSE
We prepare your body.
Think of your stem cells as seeds. Seeds grow best in healthy soil. DOX gets that soil ready. It has two parts, and both are built from your BIOBLUEPRINT results.
PART 1 · NUTRITION DETOX
A food plan made for you. It takes out what works against your body. It adds what your body needs. Our team checks in and adjusts it as you go.
PART 2 · ANF LIVER CLEANSE
A course of ANF sessions at the Centre, given by our clinical team. They run alongside your food plan to support your liver and your recovery.
At the end
We re-check key markers. You see how your body has responded. And you’re ready for the next step.
Who it’s for
Anyone preparing for CELLUTION. And anyone who wants structured help with food, lifestyle and recovery, alongside their usual medical care.
DOX is not a replacement for medical detox, treatment of poisoning, or treatment for a medical condition.

STEP 04 · CELLUTION · REGENERATIVE MEDICINE
STEP 04 · CELLUTION · REGENERATIVE MEDICINE
Your own cells, working for you.
CELLUTION is our regenerative medicine programme. It uses only your own body, in two parts, in one visit.
PART 1 · GROWTH FACTORS We take a small blood sample and separate your platelets using the SW PRP system. Platelets carry growth factors: your body’s natural repair signals.
PART 2 · BMAC We collect a small amount of your bone marrow and concentrate your stem cells, your body’s repair cells. BMAC stands for Bone Marrow Aspirate Concentrate.
NOTHING ADDED Only your own blood and your own cells. No donor cells.
NOTHING GROWN Everything is concentrated in a centrifuge. Nothing is grown or multiplied in a lab.
NOTHING ALTERED Your cells are not changed in any way. And they never leave the Centre.
Who it’s for People with joint wear (such as knee osteoarthritis), diabetes, or fertility concerns, once a specialist confirms it’s suitable.
